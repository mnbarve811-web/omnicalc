## Scope

Homepage (`/`). Visitor mode: Persuade, but the persuasion is the tool itself: most visitors want to find a calculator fast. Code-led build.

## Audience and task

Search visitors, planners and learners (PRODUCT.md). Task: find the right calculator in seconds, trust that results are real. Constraints: h1 contains "OmniCalc"; pillar titles stay visible; homepage canonical "/"; no fabricated claims or stats.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Find it, then trust it. The first viewport is a search and a real worked result, not a brand billboard. Refuses the oversized wordmark, serif accent words, eyebrows, glows, numbered pillars and decorative marquee of the previous draft.

OWN-WORLD: Same calibrated-instrument world as the calculator template: blue-tinted ground, white panels, blue-black ink, single cobalt, pillar hues as wayfinding dots only, Geist with tabular figures, radii 6/10/full, one soft panel shadow.

STORY: The visitor sees what OmniCalc covers, searches or picks a category, and sees a genuine computed example that proves results are exact and shareable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: h1 "OmniCalc" at ~56-72px with one plain sentence of what it is, then a large search field (primary action) and a row of popular calculator links. Right column: the worked compound-interest example panel computed by the real engine, bars in cobalt/slate, linking to the calculator with those inputs in the URL.

FORM: Pinned by the user (approved design system, 2026-10-31); the concept roll was skipped because the direction is user-pinned. seed: none

SIGNATURE MOVE: The worked example's yearly bars grow once on load (the page's single authored motion moment); everything else is static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
